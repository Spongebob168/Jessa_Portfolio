Zoom & Google Meet

Our group used Zoom and Google Meet to prepare for our demo teaching. Through virtual meetings, we shared ideas, assigned tasks, and worked together even at a distance. This helped us stay organized and communicate effectively. Overall, the experience showed how digital tools support teamwork, making our preparation easier and more flexible for everyone involved.

Digital Collaboration

Our group used laptops to create and organize our lesson plan. With tools like Microsoft Word and Google Docs, we were able to edit, share, and finalize our work easily. Using a laptop made our planning faster, more organized, and more efficient. Overall, it helped us collaborate better and prepare effectively for our demo teaching — demonstrating how technology removes barriers and enables seamless group work.

Microsoft PowerPoint

Our group used Microsoft PowerPoint to present our lesson during the demo teaching. It helped us organize our ideas, show visuals, and explain the topic clearly. The slides made our presentation more engaging and easier to understand. Overall, using PowerPoint improved our delivery and helped us present confidently as a group — proving that the right digital tool makes all the difference.

Key Takeaway

Digital tools don't replace teaching — they amplify it.

From virtual meetings to interactive slides, technology helped us prepare, collaborate, and deliver our demo teaching with greater confidence, creativity, and clarity than ever before.
